ABOUT THE CORNER CAFE

A welcoming corner on Waghodia Road.

The Corner Cafe is designed as a comfortable neighborhood gathering spot where good food, refreshing drinks, and relaxed conversations come together.

Located at Vaikunth Char Rasta, our menu brings together comforting cafe favorites including handcrafted Kulhad Pizza, crisp grilled sandwiches, burgers, pasta, and chilled beverages. Drop in with friends, grab a quick bite, or unwind over coffee.
