OUR STORY · THE CORNER CAFE

More than coffee. A place to belong.

VADODARA · 22.2897° N, 73.2274° E VAIKUNTH CHAR RASTA, WAGHODIA RD
✦ EST. VADODARA ✦

The Corner Cafe was created as a comfortable neighbourhood gathering spot on Waghodia Road—where good food, refreshing drinks, and relaxed conversations come together without rush.

Located at Vaikunth Char Rasta, our kitchen brings together comforting cafe favorites including handcrafted Kulhad Pizza, crisp grilled sandwiches, burgers, pasta, and chilled beverages. Drop in with friends, grab a quick bite, or unwind over coffee.
